New Here?

If this is your first lifestyle event, welcome. One Leg Up keeps things organized like a private club, while still feeling comfortable and social like a well-hosted house party.

  • Start social: Most new guests begin with conversation, drinks, and dancing.
  • Dress code: Tropical chic—linen, florals, island colors, and polished nightlife style.
  • Bring: A great attitude, tropical flair, and respect for boundaries.
  • No pressure: “No thanks” is always complete, and everyone is expected to honor it.

First-time guests can request a host introduction at check-in so you can meet people and settle in comfortably.

What to Expect at Our Parties

Size & Atmosphere

We are intentionally house-party sized (typically 30–60 guests). That means better energy, easier conversation, and a safer environment where hosts can actually support everyone present.

Professional Hosting

You will be welcomed by hosts at check-in, given a brief orientation, and introduced to the flow of the evening so you never feel lost. We prioritize comfort, clarity, and discretion.

Event Flow

Early evening is social-first: meet-and-greet, music, and mixers. As the night progresses, guests choose their own pace while hosts maintain a respectful, low-pressure environment.

Privacy & Discretion

No photos, videos, or live posting at events. Venue details are shared after RSVP approval only, and guest privacy is treated as non-negotiable.

Check-In & Verification

Guests are verified at arrival against the approved RSVP list. New attendees receive a quick rules review, host contact points, and venue flow guidance before joining the social areas.

Host Support Through the Night

Dedicated hosts circulate all evening to answer questions, facilitate introductions, and step in if any boundary or safety concern appears.

What We Expect From Members

Consent Culture

Ask first, listen fully, and respect every answer immediately. Ongoing consent matters as much as first consent.

Social Conduct

Be friendly, inclusive, and drama-free. Aggressive behavior, pressure tactics, or repeated boundary testing are grounds for removal.

Substance Responsibility

Keep it classy and in control. If someone appears overly intoxicated or unsafe, hosts intervene and participation stops.

Care for the Space

Treat the venue like a private home: clean up after yourself, respect host areas, and help preserve the quality of the experience for everyone.

Communication Standards

Be clear and direct about your boundaries and availability. Mixed signals, pressure, or repeated asks after a decline are not acceptable.

Community Reputation

We protect a trusted social circle. Guests who disregard consent, privacy, or respectful conduct are removed and may be declined from all future events.
